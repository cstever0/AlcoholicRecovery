## 1. Project Title and Overview

The **Cardiomyopathy Recovery Intelligence Platform (CRIP)** is an open, reproducible, multi-modal clinical AI research proof of concept developed to investigate recovery trajectories in non-ischemic cardiomyopathy associated with heavy alcohol exposure.

The platform bridges real cardiac magnetic resonance (CMR) imaging with synthetic clinical toxicology, genomics, and electronic health record (EHR) data to explore interpretable prediction of 6-month left ventricular systolic recovery.

```

| 🫀 Real Cardiac MRI (ACDC)       | 🍷 Toxicology Biomarkers (PEth)  | 🧬 Titin (*TTN*) Genetics  |
```

## 2. Problem Statement

Alcoholic cardiomyopathy (ACM) represents a clinically recognized form of dilated cardiomyopathy (DCM) characterized by impaired left ventricular contractility and chamber dilation resulting from chronic heavy ethanol consumption.

In clinical practice, ACM exhibits a uniquely reversible disease trajectory: complete cessation of alcohol intake often leads to substantial structural reverse remodeling and functional systolic recovery. However, clinical response remains highly heterogeneous:
- A subset of patients achieves marked recovery ($\Delta \text{LVEF} \ge 10\%$).
- Others experience persistent systolic dysfunction, refractory heart failure, or life-threatening arrhythmias despite documented sobriety.
- Subjective patient recall of drinking history is frequently unreliable, necessitating objective direct biomarkers like Phosphatidylethanol (PEth).
- Underlying genetic mutations (notably truncating variants in the giant sarcomeric protein Titin, *TTNtv*) and irreversible myocardial replacement fibrosis (late gadolinium enhancement, LGE) profoundly blunt recovery potential.

CRIP was engineered to model this clinical problem by integrating real imaging with simulated genetic and toxicological determinants.

## 3. Research Objectives

CRIP was established around four foundational research goals:

1. **Real Cardiac MRI Automation:** Ingest and process real clinical CMR scans from the Automated Cardiac Diagnosis Challenge (ACDC 2017), calculate standardized physical chamber volumetrics, and train an automated deep learning segmentation network (MONAI 2D U-Net) to quantify Left Ventricular Ejection Fraction (LVEF) within $\pm 5.0$ percentage points of expert consensus.
2. **Multimodal Synthetic Clinical Cohort:** Synthesize a literature-grounded clinical population ($N=3,000$ patients) capturing the interplay between toxic alcohol biomarkers (PEth), Titin genetics, tissue fibrosis, and longitudinal LVEF change under sustained abstinence.
3. **Interpretable Recovery Prediction:** Build and audit transparent, non-black-box predictive models (regularized Logistic Regression) providing exact additive log-odds feature attributions and linear SHAP decompositions.
4. **Clinical Research Demonstration & Interoperability:** Deliver an interactive, clinician-facing multi-page web platform featuring hypothetical scenario exploration, research reporting (Markdown, HTML, JSON), and bidirectional mock HL7 FHIR R4 bundle interoperability.

## 5. Features and Capabilities

- **High-Throughput Medical Image I/O:** SimpleITK and NiBabel pipelines parsing 3D/4D NIfTI cardiac volumes, preserving physical millimeter spacing and affine coordinate geometries.
- **Automated Deep Learning Segmentation:** Pre-configured MONAI 2D U-Net architecture predicting RV cavity, myocardium, and LV cavity directly from short-axis cine slices.
- **Physical Cardiac Volumetric Engine:** Geometric slice-by-slice voxel volume integration calculating LVEDV, LVESV, LVSV, LVEF, RVEDV, RVESV, RVSV, RVEF, and myocardial mass using the standard human myocardial tissue density constant ($\rho = 1.05\text{ g/mL}$).
- **Literature-Grounded Synthetic Clinical Database:** 3,000-subject synthetic multimodal database adhering to strict Pydantic clinical constraints ($ESV < EDV$, PEth risk bands, ACMG-AMP Titin classification).
- **Interpretable Machine Learning:** Scikit-learn recovery classification pipeline (`v1.0-m3b`) with exact additive log-odds decompositions ($\beta_j \times x_j$) and SHAP interpretability.
- **Interactive Multi-Page Streamlit Interface:** Modern 6-page UI covering platform overview, patient exploration, cine MRI segmentation inspection, dedicated biventricular cardiac measurements, multimodal recovery estimation, and scientific model evaluation.
- **Statistical Method Comparison & Bland–Altman Analysis:** Formal measurement agreement module (`crip.evaluation.measurement_agreement`) calculating mean bias, 95% limits of agreement, standard errors, and confidence intervals alongside interactive identity-line scatter plots ($y = x$).
- **Poster-Ready Figures & Artifacts:** Standalone generator exporting 8 publication-quality 300-DPI exhibits (PNG, SVG, PDF) with full provenance manifest.
- **Mock HL7 FHIR R4 Interoperability:** Bidirectional export, import, and schema validation of standard FHIR R4 bundles covering `Patient`, `Observation` (LOINC-coded), and `DiagnosticReport` resources.
- **Rigorous Automated Testing:** 296 automated pytest unit, integration, and regression tests ensuring zero mathematical contradictions, zero data leakage, and verified reproducible metrics.

## 10. Path A — Quick Start: Synthetic Data Only

> [!TIP]
> ### Two Independent Pathways to Reproduce CRIP
> - **PATH A (Quick Start: Synthetic Data Only):** Takes **3 to 5 minutes** from cloning to a fully interactive clinical dashboard. **No medical imaging downloads or challenge accounts required.** It uses deterministic synthetic clinical cohort generation ($N=3,000$ patients) and Scikit-learn recovery modeling. Fully powers the Streamlit Dashboard, Cohort Explorer, Recovery Estimator, and Evaluation metrics out of the box.
> - **PATH B (Complete MRI Multi-Dataset & Model Reproduction):** For researchers wishing to train or evaluate deep learning 2D U-Net models on **314 real cardiac MRI scans** across **ACDC 2017**, **M&Ms 2020**, and **EMIDEC 2020** (described in [Section 12](#12-path-b--complete-real-mri-dataset-acquisition-and-setup)).

### 11.3 Ubuntu on Windows Subsystem for Linux (WSL2)
1. Ensure WSL2 is up to date in Windows PowerShell: `wsl --update`
2. Launch Ubuntu inside WSL2.
3. **CRITICAL PERFORMANCE DIRECTIVE:** Clone the repository inside the native Linux filesystem (e.g. `~/projects/AlcoholicRecovery` or `/home/<user>/AlcoholicRecovery`), **NEVER** under the Windows mount `/mnt/c/`.
   > [!WARNING]

### 11.5 Hardware Acceleration & CPU Fallback
CRIP automatically probes for available compute accelerators on every training and inference invocation:
- **macOS:** Apple Silicon MPS (`mps`) device auto-selected.
- **Linux / WSL2:** NVIDIA CUDA (`cuda`) auto-selected if CUDA 12+ and compatible GPU drivers are present.
- **CPU Fallback:** If neither CUDA nor MPS is present, PyTorch and MONAI fall back smoothly to the CPU (`cpu`).
- **Benchmark Execution Times:**
  - 2D U-Net Inference: ~1 second per 3D volume on CPU; ~0.25 seconds on MPS/CUDA.
  - 2D U-Net Training (25 epochs): ~15–20 minutes on modern CPU; ~3 minutes on Apple Silicon MPS or NVIDIA GPU.

### 23.5 EMIDEC Delayed-Enhancement Tissue Characterization Benchmark

CRIP provides full support for delayed-enhancement (late gadolinium enhancement, LGE) CMR tissue characterization using the **EMIDEC** dataset (100 training cases: 33 Normal Controls, 67 Pathological Infarction Cases). 

#### Mathematical Formulation:
- **Voxel Volume:** $V_{\text{voxel}} = \frac{dx \cdot dy \cdot dz}{1000.0} \text{ mL}$
- **Total Myocardium:** $V_{\text{total\_myo}} = V_{\text{norm\_myo}} + V_{\text{infarct}} + V_{\text{no\_reflow}}$ (excludes LV blood pool)
- **Infarction Burden:** $\text{Burden}_{\text{infarct}} = \frac{V_{\text{infarct}}}{V_{\text{total\_myo}}} \times 100\%$
- **No-Reflow (MVO) Burden:** $\text{Burden}_{\text{mvo}} = \frac{V_{\text{no\_reflow}}}{V_{\text{total\_myo}}} \times 100\%$
- **Myocardial Mass:** $M_{\text{myo}} = V_{\text{total\_myo}} \times 1.05 \text{ g/mL}$

### 23.9 Unified Empirically Informed Synthetic Data and Recovery Modeling (Stage 5C)

CRIP Stage 5C establishes a mathematically rigorous, empirically grounded synthetic patient generator and recovery modeling benchmark combining clinical priors from all three cardiac MRI datasets (**ACDC**, **M&Ms**, and **EMIDEC**).

#### Key Architecture & Governance:
- **Zero Data Leakage:** Empirical Gaussian copula parameters are estimated strictly from the **240 source training cases** (80 ACDC, 80 M&Ms, 80 EMIDEC). All **74 held-out evaluation cases** are quarantined exclusively for external realism testing.
- **Physical Invariants:** 100% adherence to physical identities across all 3,000 synthetic patients ($\text{LVSV} = \text{LVEDV} - \text{LVESV}$, $\text{LVEF} = 100 \times \text{LVSV} / \text{LVEDV}$, $\text{LVESV} < \text{LVEDV}$, $\text{RVESV} < \text{RVEDV}$; 0.0% constraint violations).
- **Correlation Preservation:** 5D Gaussian copula operating in log-volume and logit-EF space preserves key clinical trade-offs ($r(\text{LVEDV}, \text{LVEF}) = -0.685$, $r(\text{LVEDV}, \text{Mass}) = +0.858$, $r(\text{LVEF}, \text{RVEF}) = +0.632$).
- **Dual Clinical Scenarios:** 75% Non-Ischemic ACM (remodeling, 0% ischemic infarct/MVO) vs 25% Ischemic Viability (grounded in EMIDEC; mean 19.5% scar burden, 62.3% MVO prevalence).
- **Immutable Preservations:** Baseline ACDC U-Net, Joint Cine U-Net, and EMIDEC LGE U-Net checkpoints remain byte-identical to original SHA-256 digests. Canonical baseline cohort (`data/synthetic/synthetic_cardiomyopathy_cohort.csv`) is preserved.

## 24. Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'crip'`
- **Cause:** The package was not installed in editable mode or the virtual environment is not activated.
- **Fix:** Activate `.venv` and run `pip install -e .` from the repository root.

### 2. Windows PowerShell Execution Policy Error
- **Error:** `File Activate.ps1 cannot be loaded because running scripts is disabled on this system.`
- **Cause:** Default Windows PowerShell security policy restricts script execution.
- **Fix:** Allow script execution for the active terminal process:
  ```powershell
  Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process -ExecutionPolicy Bypass
  .venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
  ```

### 3. Unsupported Python Version (< 3.11)
- **Error:** `ERROR: Package 'crip' requires a different Python: 3.10.x not in '>=3.11'`
- **Cause:** CRIP requires Python 3.11, 3.12, or 3.13 due to modern typing syntax and dependency constraints.
- **Fix:** Install Python 3.12 via Homebrew (`brew install python@3.12`), apt (`sudo apt-get install python3.12 python3.12-venv`), or python.org, then rebuild `.venv`.

### 4. SimpleITK NIfTI Coordinate Warning
- **Warning:** `NiftiImageIO: patientXXX_frameYY.nii.gz has unexpected scales in sform`
- **Cause:** Standard ITK notice for anisotropic voxels or minor scaling discrepancies between NIfTI `qform` and `sform` matrices.
- **Fix:** Harmless informational notice; does not affect voxel counts or volumetric math.

### 5. Matplotlib Font Cache Warning or Permission Denied
- **Warning:** `Matplotlib is building the font cache; this may take a moment` or `Path is not writable`.
- **Fix:** Set a local writable cache directory before running scripts:
  ```bash
  export MPLCONFIGDIR=/tmp/matplotlib
  ```

### 6. Streamlit Port 8501 Conflicts
- **Error:** `Port 8501 is already in use.`
- **Fix:** Launch on a different port:
  ```bash
  python -m streamlit run src/crip/app/main.py --server.port 8502
  ```

### 7. Missing ACDC Dataset in Path A vs. Path B
- **Behavior:** In Path A (Synthetic Data Only), Pages 1, 3, and 4 work completely. Pages 2 and 5 display an informative banner indicating that raw CMR scans have not been downloaded. **The application does not crash.**
- **Fix to Enable Page 2 & 5:** Follow [Path B (Section 12)](#12-path-b--complete-real-mri-dataset-acquisition-and-setup) to download and extract the dataset into `data/raw/acdc/training`.

### 8. Missing MRI Checkpoints
- **Error:** `FileNotFoundError: Checkpoint not found at models/mri/best_unet_model.pt`
- **Behavior:** If deep learning weights are not present, Page 2 falls back to displaying expert ground-truth contours.
- **Fix:** Train the baseline U-Net (`python src/crip/mri/train_segmentation.py --epochs 25`) or joint U-Net (`python -m crip.pipeline train --task cine`).

### 9. Missing Recovery Model Checkpoint
- **Behavior:** If `models/recovery/recovery_pipeline.joblib` is missing, Streamlit will automatically train and serialize the default model on startup. Alternatively, run `python -m crip.modeling.train`.

### 10. PyTorch CUDA / MPS Device Fallback
- **Notice:** `Using CPU device.`
- **Fix:** Normal on systems without dedicated GPUs. To force CPU execution explicitly:
  ```bash
  python src/crip/mri/train_segmentation.py --device cpu
  ```

### 11. WSL2 Localhost Connection from Windows Host
- **Issue:** Unable to open `http://localhost:8501` from Windows host browser.
- **Fix:** Ensure WSL2 is using mirrored networking or launch Streamlit binding to all addresses:
  ```bash
  python -m streamlit run src/crip/app/main.py --server.address 0.0.0.0
  ```

## 25. Reproducibility and Limitations

### Computational Reproducibility
- All synthetic cohort generation, dataset splitting, U-Net training, and Scikit-learn pipelines use fixed seeds (seed 42).
- Minor variations in floating-point outputs may arise across different hardware backends (e.g., Apple Silicon MPS vs. NVIDIA CUDA vs. CPU vectorization).

### Scientific and Clinical Limitations
1. **Synthetic Outcomes:** Recovery endpoints ($\Delta \text{LVEF} \ge 10.0\%$) and biomarker concentrations are generated computationally from published empirical distributions. They do not constitute real clinical prospective data.
2. **ACDC Cohort Scope:** The public ACDC dataset contains cases of dilated cardiomyopathy, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, myocardial infarction, and right ventricular abnormalities. It does not contain clinically verified alcoholic cardiomyopathy cases.
3. **No Clinical Reference Consensus:** All ACDC volume measurements are calculated via geometric voxel integration of expert segmentation masks. The challenge did not provide separate core-lab tabulated consensus scalar values.
